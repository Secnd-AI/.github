# Secnd

We're an AI research lab building decision models, and products on top of them.

A decision model takes a situation and a list of options, picks one and says how sure it is. When it
isn't sure, it asks.

## Models

**Ren** is our first model. It works from option names alone and gets better with a few examples.

[Weights](https://huggingface.co/Secnd-AI/ren/tree/v1.1.0) ·
[Code](https://github.com/Secnd-AI/ren) ·
[Results](https://github.com/Secnd-AI/ren/tree/main/docs/results)

## Products

**Secnd** is a reflection of you. It connects to your work tools, learns how you decide, acts when
it's sure and asks when it isn't. It's in development.
