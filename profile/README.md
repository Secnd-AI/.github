# Secnd

## Philosophy

So much of what makes your work yours is the way you decide: what you approve, what you push back
on, what you let wait. It takes years to learn, and it's the hardest part of you to hand to anyone.

We believe a model can learn it. Software should be able to carry your judgment, act on it when it's
sure, and come back to you when it isn't.

Knowing when to ask matters as much as knowing the answer. A model that can't say "I don't know"
can't be left alone, however often it's right. Trust has to be earned one decision at a time, with
proof you can check.

## Vision

A second you, in every part of your work. A reflection of how you think, for every person, team and
industry, that keeps things moving while you sleep and leaves you the decisions only you can make.

We train models built for this, and everything we make stands on them.
